Leor Design site privacy and enquiry notes

- First-party privacy choices are stored in a cookie, with local storage fallback.
- The site does not collect visit or interaction analytics.
- UTM values and WhatsApp-click state are retained in session storage only for customer enquiry attribution.
- Contact and estimator submissions require customer consent and use POST /api/enquiry.
- The enquiry endpoint saves to the existing 21-column Google Sheets Leads structure using the existing Apps Script URL and webhook secret.
- Lead IDs and idempotency protection remain enabled.
- No database or third-party analytics service is used.

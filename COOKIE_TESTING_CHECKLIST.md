# Privacy choice and enquiry checklist

- [ ] The privacy banner appears when no choice has been saved.
- [ ] Continue and Essential only dismiss the banner and save the choice.
- [ ] Privacy choices opens the preferences dialog.
- [ ] Escape and the close button close the dialog; the choice is not changed by Cancel.
- [ ] Saving preferences dismisses the banner and persists the choice.
- [ ] The preferences state persists after a refresh.
- [ ] The privacy notice says the site does not collect visit analytics.
- [ ] The consultation form blocks submission until customer consent is checked.
- [ ] The estimator form blocks submission until customer consent is checked.
- [ ] Valid contact and estimator submissions still post to `/api/enquiry`.
- [ ] No analytics request is made on page load, navigation, scroll, or form interaction.

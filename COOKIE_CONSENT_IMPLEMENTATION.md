# Cookie and privacy choices

The site uses essential first-party storage to remember the privacy choice and keep core features working. It does not collect visit or interaction analytics, and it does not send analytics requests.

The preference is stored in a first-party cookie for up to 180 days, with local storage as a fallback. UTM campaign values and a WhatsApp-click marker can be kept in session storage for inclusion in a customer enquiry. They are sent only as part of a lead submitted to `/api/enquiry`.

Customer enquiry consent is separate from the cookie preference. The contact and estimator forms require that consent before submitting lead details to the Google Apps Script Leads integration.
